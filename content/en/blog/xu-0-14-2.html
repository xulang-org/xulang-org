Xu 0.14.2 Released: 128-bit SLP Vectorization, Ext-TSP Layout, SwissTable Runtime, #core/elf, and MRT GPU

2026-11-26 · Xu v0.14.2

Xu 0.14.2 shipped on 2026-11-26 with advances across compiler auto-vectorization and code layout, runtime data structures, bare-metal kernel and ELF primitives, and #tensor storage plus modern GPU rendering.

1. Compiler Vectorization, Layout & Optimizer

  • 128-bit Intra-Block SLP Auto-Vectorization (opt_slp): Packs unrolled isomorphic array memory/arithmetic trees and 4-way associative integer reductions into 128-bit SIMD instructions on ARM64 NEON and baseline x86-64 SSE2 while preserving strict left-to-right IEEE-754 evaluation semantics (disable via XU_NO_SLP=1).
  • Ext-TSP Layout & Correlated Jump Threading: Replaces greedy trace layout in opt_cfg with Wu–Larus edge-weight chain merging (cfg_exttsp_hot_order) and loop rotation, alongside dominator/range-driven jump threading (opt_jump_thread, disable via XU_NO_JT=1).
  • Regalloc & Scalar Pipeline: Adds interval hole reuse, Ball–Larus call-site weighting, cold-call caller-save prioritization, zero-spill constant/MI_ADDR rematerialization, GVN cross-call reuse, scope-disjoint LICM, SROA (opt_sroa), and MIR lowering for float64_bits/float64_from_bits.

2. SwissTable Runtime & Bare-Metal #core Substrate

  • SwissTable Hash Maps (core/map): Rebuilds the runtime map around a compact 8-slot SWAR (CTRL_LANE_MUL) control-byte probe design (7/8 load factor), alongside machine-word scanning improvements in core/chan, compress/lzw, and core/intrin.
  • Freestanding & Multi-Section ELF: Adds usize/isize, packed/union/bitfield C structs, freestanding sections (.bss/.data/.rodata/@section/@align/@export), sizeof/alignof/offsetof, static_assert, @noreturn, @panic_handler, cpuid/syscall4 intrinsics, and multi-section ET_EXEC/ET_REL ELF emission for x64-none, arm64-none, and riscv64-none.
  • New #core Modules: Adds #core/addr, #core/ds, #core/elf, #core/fmt, and #core/mmio, and expands #core/virtio with multi-sector virtio-blk reads.

3. #tensor, Modern GPU & Hostless #ui/widgets

  • Low-Precision Tensor Foundations: Introduces src/tensor (float, quant, view) and signed P4K4 Q4/Q8 kernels, M=12 GEMM, and paged SIMD attention kernels.
  • Modern GPU & Hostless UI: Adds R32_UINT, MRT begin_render_targets, storage buffer/texture bindings, and indirect draw/dispatch to #gpu/SDL, plus hostless Screen/Context support and TreeView multi-selection fixes in #ui/widgets.
