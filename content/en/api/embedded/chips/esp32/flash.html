Fixed-profile bond persistence

The two-slot backend is specific to a 4 MiB ESP32 layout. It validates partition ownership and refuses encrypted flash. Stop radio connections/advertising and the APP CPU before writes, and keep the complete cache-off call graph in IRAM or ROM. Records contain plaintext keys and must not be logged. Restoring bond keys does not restore an encrypted link or subscribed client configuration.
