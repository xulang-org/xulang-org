Explicit 1D/2D tile movement, reduction, and matrix multiplication primitives. On hosted arm64 and x64 targets at -O1+, `gemm_i64` and canonical 1D loops are automatically lowered by `MirTilePlan` (`tile_gemm`) and the whole-loop SIMD matchers into L1-blocked 2D register-tile and vector micro-kernels; all other targets and -O0 execute the portable reference loops below with identical bounds and wrapping semantics.
