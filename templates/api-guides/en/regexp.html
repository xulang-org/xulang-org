Matching, finding, splitting, expansion and replacement use a shared work budget and explicit heap stacks. Handle errors from these operations, for example matched := re.match_string(&text) or (err) { << 1 }. Budget exhaustion is an error; a valid nonmatch retains its empty or false result. Expressions are limited to 65,536 bytes and 64 nested groups. quote_meta, num_subexp and literal_prefix do not perform matching.
