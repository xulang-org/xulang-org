Full-width unsigned arithmetic

mul64 and div64 interpret int inputs as unsigned 64-bit bit patterns, including negative signed representations. Multiplication returns the low lane in Carry.lo and the high lane in Carry.carry. Division consumes the full 128-bit pair (hi, lo), returning quotient and remainder in those fields. Zero divisors and quotients that do not fit 64 bits are errors. These software operations do not provide a constant-time guarantee.
