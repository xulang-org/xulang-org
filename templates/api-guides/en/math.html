Rounding and special values

Xu 0.11.3 rounding preserves already-integral binary64 values near 252, signed zero and non-finite inputs. round rounds ties away from zero; round_to_even resolves ties to an even integer. log(±0) returns negative infinity, log(+Inf) returns positive infinity, and frexp returns zero, infinity or NaN in its fraction with exponent zero. These boundary repairs do not establish accuracy guarantees for every finite transcendental approximation.
