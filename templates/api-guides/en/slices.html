Built-in slice methods · Xu 0.14.1

The methods below belong to mutable dynamic slices and are available since Xu 0.14.1. Call them directly on a mutable slice or through a writable borrow.

slice.reserve(n int)

Ensures capacity for at least n elements while preserving the length and existing values. n is the total requested capacity. Pass a nonnegative count.

slice.resize_zero(n int)

Sets the slice length to n. Growth adds zero values and preserves the existing prefix. Shrinking keeps the capacity and the prefix of length n. A negative n leaves the slice as it was. Available for integer, floating-point, and boolean element types. See the local inference guide for related APIs.
